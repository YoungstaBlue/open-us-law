### C. Controlling case law by claim type

**Suppressed / withheld evidence**
- *Brady v. Maryland*, 373 U.S. 83 (1963) — suppression of material exculpatory evidence violates due process
- *Giglio v. United States*, 405 U.S. 150 (1972) — extends Brady to impeachment evidence and witness deals
- *Kyles v. Whitley*, 514 U.S. 419 (1995) — prosecutor's duty extends to evidence known only to police

**False warrant / false affidavit**
- *Franks v. Delaware*, 438 U.S. 154 (1978) — right to a hearing where an affidavit contains a knowing or reckless falsehood
- *Malley v. Briggs*, 475 U.S. 335 (1986) — no immunity for an officer who obtains a warrant no reasonable officer would seek

**Force / arrest**
- *Graham v. Connor*, 490 U.S. 386 (1989) — excessive force judged by Fourth Amendment objective reasonableness
- *Tennessee v. Garner*, 471 U.S. 1 (1985) — deadly force limits
- *Gerstein v. Pugh*, 420 U.S. 103 (1975) and *County of Riverside v. McLaughlin*, 500 U.S. 44 (1991) — probable-cause determination generally required within 48 hours of warrantless arrest

**Jail conditions / medical care**
- *Estelle v. Gamble*, 429 U.S. 97 (1976) — deliberate indifference to serious medical needs
- *Farmer v. Brennan*, 511 U.S. 825 (1994) — deliberate-indifference standard defined
- *Bell v. Wolfish*, 441 U.S. 520 (1979) — pretrial detainees may not be punished at all
- *Kingsley v. Hendrickson*, 576 U.S. 389 (2015) — pretrial detainee force claims use an objective standard

**Counsel**
- *Gideon v. Wainwright*, 372 U.S. 335 (1963) — right to appointed counsel
- *Strickland v. Washington*, 466 U.S. 668 (1984) — ineffective assistance standard
- *United States v. Cronic*, 466 U.S. 648 (1984) — prejudice presumed where counsel entirely fails to test the prosecution's case

**Court access / retaliation**
- *Bounds v. Smith*, 430 U.S. 817 (1977) and *Lewis v. Casey*, 518 U.S. 343 (1996) — right of access to the courts
- *Hartman v. Moore*, 547 U.S. 250 (2006) — retaliatory prosecution
- *Nieves v. Bartlett*, 587 U.S. 391 (2019) — retaliatory arrest

**Municipal / supervisory liability (the "pattern" argument)**
- *Monell v. Dep't of Social Services*, 436 U.S. 658 (1978) — liability where a policy or custom caused the violation
- *City of Canton v. Harris*, 489 U.S. 378 (1989) — failure to train as deliberate indifference
- *Connick v. Thompson*, 563 U.S. 51 (2011) — the high bar the pattern must clear (know the obstacle)
